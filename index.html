Why Mind Mirror

A culturally aware mental health companion in your pocket.

Built for Canada’s diverse communities, Mind Mirror is designed to make mental health, cognitive wellness, and emotional support more accessible, personalized, and stigma-free.

Platform

One connected ecosystem for mental wellness.

24/7 AI emotional support

Personalized support for stress, anxiety, burnout, and everyday emotional well-being.

Burnout detection

Early insights from stress, sleep, activity, and wellness patterns to help prevent escalation.

Cognitive wellness insights

Support for early cognitive health awareness, caregiver alerts, and proactive well-being guidance.

Chronic illness emotional care

Emotional mapping for people managing diabetes, heart disease, chronic pain, cancer, or other long-term conditions.

Multilingual support

Designed for English, French, Hindi, Punjabi, and Mandarin-speaking communities.

Human care escalation

Connect users to therapists, crisis lines, and healthcare resources when human support is needed.
