Why Mind Mirror

A culturally aware mental health companion in your pocket.

Built for Canada’s diverse communities, Mind Mirror is designed to make mental health, cognitive wellness, and emotional support more accessible, personalized, and stigma-free.

How it helps

AI-guided support, with a path to human connection.

Mind Mirror is being designed to help people check in with their emotional wellbeing, recognize patterns, and take the next right step — whether that is self-reflection, a coping exercise, family support, workplace resources, or human care.

1. Check in

Share how you are feeling, what is causing stress, or what kind of support you need.

2. Reflect with guidance

Mind Mirror helps you organize your thoughts, identify emotional patterns, and understand what may be affecting your wellbeing.

3. Receive practical next steps

Get gentle prompts, coping suggestions, journaling guidance, and wellbeing resources based on your needs.

4. Connect to human support when needed

Mind Mirror is not meant to replace human care. The long-term vision includes pathways to connect with family, community resources, professionals, or employer wellness programs.
