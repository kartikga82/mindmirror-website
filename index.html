A few things to know

Good questions.

Who can join the launch community?

Adults of all ages across Canada are welcome. Our planned programs for ages 18–34 are a particular focus, not an age restriction on the community. You do not need to provide your age when signing up.

Why a particular focus on young adults?

Statistics Canada’s analysis of 2022 data found that some of the largest increases in mood and anxiety disorders over the previous decade were among young people, particularly women aged 15–24. This supports paying particular attention to younger people alongside adults at other life stages. Mind Mirror’s planned 18–34 programs are our chosen focus; that age range is not the study’s reported age group.

Read the Statistics Canada study summary (2023).

Can I use Mind Mirror today?

The product is in early development. This signup is for the launch community, research invitations, and updates. A public launch date has not been announced.

What am I committing to?

You’re signing up for emails. Any research or prototype session is optional, and you’ll receive details before deciding whether to participate.

Does it cost anything to join?

No payment is required to join the launch community. Future product and pilot pricing have not been announced.

Is Mind Mirror therapy?

No. We’re exploring an everyday reflection and mental wellness tool. Mind Mirror is not a clinical service and does not provide diagnosis, treatment, or crisis support.

Can my workplace take part?

We’re also speaking with small and medium-sized employers in tech, healthcare, and finance about potential pilots. Explore the employer page.
